## 练习：读标题猜方向

先遮住右边，看标题判断：利好还是利空？幅度大还是小？

| 标题 | 答案 |
|---|---|
| *Nvidia shares soar after revenue tops estimates, guidance beats* | 利好，大涨。营收超预期、指引也超预期 |
| *Target tumbles as retailer slashes full-year outlook* | 利空，大跌。下调全年展望 |
| *Stocks edge up ahead of Fed decision* | 小涨，市场在等美联储 |
| *Treasury yields jump after hot CPI print stokes inflation fears* | 国债收益率大涨，通胀数据超预期，对股市（尤其科技股）偏利空 |
| *Apple slips despite earnings beat as China sales disappoint* | 小跌。财报超预期，但中国销售让人失望 |
| *Fed holds rates steady, signals two cuts later this year* | 偏利好，按兵不动但暗示降息 |
| *Tesla plunges after deliveries miss, Musk warns on margins* | 利空，暴跌。交付量不及预期，利润率预警 |
| *Market reaction muted as results come in line* | 反应平淡，业绩符合预期 |
